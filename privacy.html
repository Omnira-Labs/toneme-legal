Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Attesto ("the App") lets you create a permanent, tamper-proof record of a file, by storing it on Walrus, a decentralized storage network. This policy explains what we do and don't do with your information.

What we collect

When you create a real (paid) proof, the file you select is uploaded to Walrus. Once uploaded, this file is permanent and cannot be deleted by you, by us, or by anyone — this is deliberate, and is what makes a proof trustworthy. Files used in Demo Mode never leave your device and are never stored anywhere.

What we don't do

We don't store your files ourselves — real proofs go directly to Walrus, and demo files never leave your device. We don't sell your data. Your Proof History (file names, fingerprints, and links) is stored locally on your device only.

Third parties we use

Walrus, a decentralized storage network, permanently stores files submitted as real proofs. Our backend (Cloudflare) routes requests securely and keeps minimal technical logs that are not linked to file content. Apple processes payment for real proofs via In-App Purchase; we never see your payment details.

Your rights

Because real proofs are permanently and publicly stored on Walrus by design, we cannot delete a real proof's content on your behalf once created. You can clear your local Proof History at any time from Settings.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected with a new "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: reach us through the app's App Store support page.
