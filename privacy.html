Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Toneme ("the App") helps you analyze messages and get suggested replies. This policy explains what we do and don't do with your information.

What we collect

The message text you type or paste, sent only to generate a suggested reply. Your selected reply style and relationship context are stored on your device only.

What we don't do

We don't store your messages after a reply is generated. We don't read other messaging apps automatically — you choose what to paste here. We don't sell your data.

Third parties we use

A third-party AI language model provider processes your message text to generate replies — see that provider's own privacy policy for their data retention practices. Our backend routes requests securely and keeps minimal technical logs for security purposes that automatically expire within hours.

Your rights

Because we don't keep a persistent copy of your messages, there's typically no message content to access, correct, or delete on our end.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected with a new "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: reach us through the app's App Store support page.
