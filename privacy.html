Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Toneme ("the App") helps you analyze messages and get suggested replies. This policy explains what we do and don't do with your information.

What we collect

The message text you type or paste, sent only to generate a suggested reply. Your selected reply style and relationship context are stored on your device only. If you subscribe to Premium, each time your subscription is confirmed active we keep a technical record (a one-way hash of the transaction ID, plus expiry date) for 180 days — no personal information, used solely to resolve payment disputes.

What we don't do

We don't store your messages after a reply is generated. We don't read other messaging apps automatically — you choose what to paste here. We don't sell your data.

Third parties we use

A third-party AI processing service generates replies from your message text — this provider never receives anything that identifies you personally (no name, account, or contact details). Our backend infrastructure routes requests securely and keeps minimal technical logs that automatically expire within hours. If you subscribe to Premium, Apple processes your payment via In-App Purchase — we never see your payment card details.

Legal basis for processing (EU/UK)

We process your message text under performance of a contract (the analysis you're requesting) and legitimate interest for security logs. You may stop processing anytime by not using the app.

International data transfer

Our processing and infrastructure providers may process data outside your country, including the US. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your rights

By design, this app doesn't keep a persistent, identifiable copy of your messages, and doesn't use accounts or user IDs — so in practice, there's rarely anything tied to you for us to locate, correct, or delete. The only data held at all is a brief technical log (IP address, timestamp) that isn't linked to message content and expires automatically within hours, with no action needed from you.

EU/UK users retain the usual rights under GDPR. California users retain rights under the CCPA/CPRA — we do not sell or share personal information.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected with a new "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: reach us through the app's App Store support page.
